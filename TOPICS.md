## Social preview

**Settings → General → Social preview → Upload an image.** GitHub wants
**1280×640 px** (2:1), PNG or JPG, under 1 MB. It is what renders when the
repo is linked on X, Slack, Discord or LinkedIn; without one they show a
generic grey card.

Nothing in the repo depends on it and no code needs to change.
