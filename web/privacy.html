Privacy and terms

This site

No cookies. One outside script host: the Prove page loads the GSAP animation library from cdnjs, pinned by its hash; it sends nothing of yours. To learn why people stay or leave, each page sends Knos small events of an anonymous visit: a random id your browser makes and resets every day, the page, each press and how long its answer took, whether you got an answer, ran a proof, backed a claim or downloaded code, three quick presses on one spot, an error shown, a tap on "Not what I need" and the word you add to it, and the last page and its seconds before you leave. No IP address, account or cookie is stored with them. If your browser sends Do Not Track, nothing is sent. /status shows the totals. The question or paper link you type goes to the Knos server, which reads the paper's title from its public record (Crossref) and answers. Your browser also reads NEAR testnet's public record.

The server

No account and no sign-in. It counts requests per address only to limit them. Every proof and payment is public on NEAR testnet.
