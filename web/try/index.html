How is the target worked out?

From the launch city's population and Qloo's mean affinity there: population over 100,000 times the affinity, never under 10.

Idea
a graphene-coated cycling jacket
City
Boulder, CO, the metro with the highest mean affinity on Qloo's heatmap (filter.type=urn:heatmap)
Population
326,831 (Census, 2023)

target = max(10, round(POP / 100,000 × mean affinity))

= max(10, 3) = 10 pre-orders, with Qloo's mean affinity (kept on the app's server)

What does this not establish?

Qloo's figures are aggregate affinities of many people, never a person's behaviour. This is directional demand evidence, not a sales forecast. How Knos uses Qloo safely
