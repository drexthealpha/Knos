Pricing

Free check. At mainnet the funder pays 1% on top of each payout; the solver keeps 100%.

While Knos runs on devnet test money, nothing real is charged.

Worked example: a funder holds 25.00 test USDC on a milestone and pays a 0.075 fee on top (0.30%, above the 0.05 floor), 25.075 in all. On proof the solver receives 25.00. At mainnet the same payout carries a 0.25 fee.

The fee field in the payment program on devnet reads FEE_BPS = 30: 0.30% of each payout, at least 0.05 test units, paid in test units worth nothing. It becomes 1% when the program is upgraded for mainnet.
