The five parts

  1. Money held: the funder's test tokens wait in the NEAR contract.
  2. Criteria hash: the brief's criteria, locked at funding; a changed brief does not match.
  3. GitHub's signed proof: an OIDC token from the locked workflow, signed with RS256.
  4. The NEAR check: signature, issuer, audience, repository, workflow, commit, expiry and a one-time id, in the contract.
  5. Chain Signatures payout: the MPC network signs the Solana transfer the contract checked.
