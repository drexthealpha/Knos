# knos-labs

Archived. knos-labs is a frozen record of experiments from Knos 0.3.9. Nothing here is maintained, and its scheduled
jobs are stopped. Its files and history are kept in Knos, under `repos/knos-labs`.

Knos lives at https://github.com/drexthealpha/Knos, and its site is https://drexthealpha.github.io/Knos/ .
